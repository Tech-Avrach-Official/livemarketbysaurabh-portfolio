01 — Inside

What actually happens inside

Not a broadcast channel. A room where people talk back — and the rhythm is the same every week, so people plan around it.

  1. Mon–Fri · 8:30

    Pre-market thread opens. Levels go up for discussion — Saurabh's read included, not handed down as a tip.

  2. 9:15 – 15:30

    Live hours. Running commentary, questions, and trades put up to be sanity-checked before they're taken.

  3. After close

    Review. Trades posted and picked apart — what worked, what didn't, and why. Losses included.

  4. Saturday

    Weekly outlook, and the discussions worth re-reading from the week.

  5. Sunday

    Doubt-clearing. Beginners get the floor and nobody gets mocked for asking.

And running underneath all of it — memes, banter, Friday chaos. Half the reason people stay.

05 — Fit

Who this is for

Join if

  • You trade, or you're learning seriously.
  • You want to discuss and improve, not just receive.
  • You can give as well as take.
  • You're fine being disagreed with.

Don't join if

  • You want guaranteed tips.
  • You want buy/sell calls to follow blindly.
  • You're here to promote your own service.
  • You expect profit without doing the work.

House rules: no tips-begging · no spam or promotions · no abuse · nothing here is financial advice.
