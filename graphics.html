Graphics I can author in vector

Every mark on this page was written by hand as SVG paths, SVG filters, or CSS gradients — no image files, no generated art. All of it scales, themes, and animates, and adds a few KB rather than a few hundred.

01 — Lit metal

SVG gradients plus a specular lighting filter. The highlight is computed from a light position, which is why the bar reads as a solid object rather than a shape with a gradient on it.

02 — Bull and bear

Line art rather than filled silhouettes: strokes stay legible at the low opacities these are used at, where a fill turns to mud. Drawn as a matched pair so they can sit together — both are the market, not good and bad.

03 — Surfaces and type

Brushed metal plates and foil lettering, both pure CSS. The foil sheen is one animated gradient clipped to the glyphs — no images, and it costs nothing to change the words.

Worth saying plainly: screenshots do not work in this environment, so I cannot see any of this rendered — I authored it from how SVG and CSS behave, not by looking at it.
